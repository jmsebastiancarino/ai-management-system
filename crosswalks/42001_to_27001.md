# Crosswalk: ISO/IEC 42001 (AIMS) ↔ ISO/IEC 27001 (ISMS)

The single most common mistake organizations make with AI governance is building it as a separate island instead of extending the ISMS they already run. ISO/IEC 42001 deliberately shares the Annex-SL management-system backbone with ISO/IEC 27001 — the same structure for context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, and improvement. The *information security* controls live in 27001 Annex A; the *AI-specific* controls live in 42001 Annex A. Where they touch, you map once and reuse.

| Catalog ID | Control | ISO 42001 | ISO 27001:2022 | Relationship |
|------------|---------|-----------|----------------|--------------|
| AIMS-GOV-1 | AI Policy and Accountability | Clause 5.2, A.2.x | A.5.1 (policies) | AI policy extends the existing security-policy framework |
| AIMS-OVS-1 | Human Oversight of AI Decisions | A.9.2, A.9.3 | A.5.1 | New AI obligation; governed under existing policy structure |
| AIMS-INC-1 | AI Incident Response and Reporting | A.10.2, A.10.3 | A.5.24, A.5.26 | AI incidents handled through the existing IR process, extended |
| AIMS-DATA-1 | Training Data Governance | A.7.x | A.5.12, A.8.10 (info classification, deletion) | *inherits* — training data governed under existing data controls + AI specifics |
| AIMS-MON-1 | Operational Monitoring & Drift | A.9.3, A.9.4 | A.8.15, A.8.16 (logging, monitoring) | *inherits* — AI monitoring extends existing logging/monitoring |

**How to read "inherits":** existing ISMS controls are not rewritten for AI — the AIMS *points at them* and layers AI-specific obligations on top (e.g. logging that captures model version and input provenance; data controls that distinguish training-data access from inference access).

**The practical payoff:** an organization already certified to ISO/IEC 27001 is not starting from zero on 42001. This crosswalk makes the reuse explicit and shows a 42001 assessor exactly where the ISMS ends and the AIMS begins — which is the first question they ask.

*ISO Annex A references are summarized, not quoted. Mappings are illustrative and synthetic.*
